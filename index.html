Request for Immediate Suspension of Petrobras’ License for Phase 4 of the Pre-Salt

Our BMI highlight this month is the discussion around Petrobras’ license for Phase 4 of the pre-salt project. In June, the Federal Prosecution Office (MPF) and the São Paulo State Prosecution Office (MP-SP) requested the immediate suspension of the license, citing a lack of evidence of the project’s environmental viability. The project calls for the installation of ten platforms in the Santos Basin. In our view, if the dispute moves forward or drags on, there is potential for delays in Petrobras’ CAPEX schedule in the region, especially in investments tied to new production units, subsea infrastructure, and field development. The Búzios field breaks its all-time record and reaches 1.2 million barrels per day with the expansion of the pre-salt layer

With the expansion of activities at the P-78 and P-79 platforms, just three days after breaking the production record of 1 million and 100 thousand barrels per day, the Búzios field, in the pre-salt layer of the Santos Basin, surpassed its own record and, on Friday (June 26), reached an average daily production of 1,200,000 barrels of oil per day. The platforms in question are currently being developed to reach their maximum capacity (ramp-up) of 180,000 bpd each. In addition to these two, the Búzios field has six other active production units. The goal, however, is for the operation to reach 12 FPSOs. The FPSOs P-80, P-82, and P-83 are still under construction; and the Búzios 12 unit is currently out for bid, providing excellent business opportunities.

ANP Releases List of Eligible Companies for Upcoming Oil and Gas Auctions

On June 26, the National Agency of Petroleum, Natural Gas, and Biofuels (ANP) released the official list of companies registered and eligible to participate in the upcoming cycles of the Permanent Offer. In total, the regulatory agency has identified 19 companies with active registrations for the 4th Cycle of the Permanent Production Sharing Offer (OPP4) and 46 companies with active registrations for the 6th Cycle of the Permanent Concession Offer (OPC6). This announcement marks an importante step in the organization of the upcoming hydrocarbon exploration and production auctions in the country.

Registration does not necessarily mean that a company will participate in the cycle or submit bids; it merely indicates that they are eligible to do so. Both cycles are scheduled for October 7, 2026.

The Permanent Production Sharing Offer aims to award contracts, under the production sharing regime, for oil and natural gas exploration and production activities in blocks. For the OPP, the notice includes 23 blocks in the Campos and Santos sedimentary basins. By August 6, the ANP will announce which of these blocks have received expressions of interest from companies and, consequently, will be offered during the public session of the 4th Cycle of the OPP.

Under the OPC, the concession notice is extensive and offers 495 exploration blocks and five marginal accumulation areas, distributed across 38 sectors in 11 Brazilian sedimentary basins.
